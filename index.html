Prototype Rules

  • This prototype uses a 4-player, 56-card Rook-style deck.
  • Each player receives 14 cards.
  • The Rook card is included as a special high card.
  • For now, the prototype skips bidding and lets you play tricks.
  • Play one card when it is your turn. The highest card in the lead color wins the trick.
  • The Rook wins the trick in this prototype.

This is a foundation for the eventual online version, not a complete implementation of every Rook ruleset.
